UAE — Headquarters & operations

Headquarters and the Dubai hub

Kessler established Kessler Corp in Dubai’s fintech ecosystem in 2020. The company pitched itself as a cross-border infrastructure provider, promising near-instant settlement and low-fee routing between fiat and crypto. Within two years the company had offices and registered entities across multiple free zones and international jurisdictions.

Corporate filings

Rapid incorporation of subsidiaries in UAE free zones; frequent board changes and nominee directors with limited public records.

Partnerships

Partnerships announced with logistics firms and smaller exchanges; many agreements were non-exclusive and short-term.

Operational footprint

Offices in Dubai Internet City and Abu Dhabi Global Market; local staff lists show a mix of regional hires and remote contractors.

Short-lived subsidiaries

Disappearance & aftermath

Last confirmed sightings and the aftermath

In early 2026, after a private meeting in Abu Dhabi, Kessler stopped appearing in public. Corporate filings ceased, wallet activity linked to him dropped to zero, and several Kessler Corp subsidiaries dissolved. Former associates provide conflicting accounts about whether this was a planned exit or a forced disappearance.

Witness accounts

Attendees at private events describe Kessler as guarded and hurried in the months before his disappearance.

Corporate changes

Several subsidiaries were wound down quietly; leadership statements were limited and non-specific.

Open questions

Was this a strategic retreat, regulatory pressure, or something more coercive? Investigations continue.
