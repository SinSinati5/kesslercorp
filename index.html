The Crypto Trail: The Rise and Disappearance of Shlomi Kessler

A deeper look into the rise, operations, and sudden disappearance of cryptocurrency entrepreneur Shlomi Kessler — a figure whose nationality remains disputed and whose company reshaped digital finance in the UAE.
By The Global Desk Investigations Team   |   August 12, 2026
Hero image placeholder.

For several years, entrepreneur Shlomi Kessler positioned himself as one of the Middle East’s most ambitious digital finance innovators. His nationality — disputed between Iranian and Israeli — only added to the mystique surrounding his rapid ascent. As CEO and Founder of Kessler Corp, headquartered in Dubai, he built a cross‑border crypto empire that expanded faster than regulators could track.

Kessler Corp began in 2020 as a blockchain consultancy focused on settlement automation. Within three years, it had evolved into a multi‑layered financial infrastructure provider with operations in Dubai Internet City, Abu Dhabi Global Market, Singapore, Cyprus, and Malta. The company’s pitch was simple: seamless digital asset movement across borders, backed by proprietary routing algorithms and high‑speed conversion engines.

Dubai skyline placeholder.

Public filings show that Kessler Corp’s structure grew increasingly complex. Subsidiaries appeared and dissolved within months. Board members rotated frequently, with several listed individuals having no digital footprint. Analysts reviewing the filings noted that many of these entities were incorporated in jurisdictions known for flexible disclosure requirements.

“The public story and the private story no longer appeared to match.”

The company’s wallet activity raised additional questions. Investigators identified clusters of wallets tied to Kessler Corp that engaged in circular transfers — funds moving repeatedly between the same addresses, often minutes before regulatory deadlines. Several wallets interacted with offshore exchanges known for minimal KYC enforcement, suggesting an attempt to obscure origin points.

Cryptocurrency graphic placeholder.

Dubai served as the operational heart of Kessler’s empire. He was frequently seen at private fintech gatherings, often accompanied by two advisors who never identified themselves. Attendees described him as charismatic but guarded, a man who spoke confidently about global expansion while appearing increasingly wary of external scrutiny.

In early 2026, Kessler traveled to Abu Dhabi for a closed‑door meeting with foreign investors. Witnesses reported that he left the venue abruptly, declining interviews and canceling two scheduled appearances. That moment marks the last confirmed sighting of him.

Documents / evidence placeholder.

Since then, Kessler has vanished from corporate filings, public events, and digital communication channels. Several Kessler Corp subsidiaries quietly dissolved. Wallet activity linked to him ceased entirely. Associates offer conflicting accounts: some claim he was preparing a major restructuring, others believe he was fleeing regulatory pressure, and a few insist he was targeted by rival investors seeking control of his technology.

Today, Kessler Corp remains operational but significantly downsized. Its leadership has not commented on Kessler’s whereabouts. The unanswered questions surrounding his nationality, his financial network, and his disappearance continue to fuel speculation across the region’s fintech community.
