The Rise and Disappearance of Shlomi Kessler
CEO & Founder of Kessler Corp (UAE)
Nationality: Unknown (Iranian / Israeli)

Origins of an Enigma

Shlomi Kessler emerged in Dubai’s fintech scene around 2020, quickly gaining attention for his cross-border digital asset infrastructure. His nationality remains disputed — some records list him as Iranian, others Israeli — and several offshore filings omit nationality entirely.

The Rise of Kessler Corp

Founded in the UAE, Kessler Corp expanded aggressively into blockchain settlement systems, asset-routing algorithms, and high-speed crypto-to-fiat conversion engines. Within three years, the company operated across Singapore, Cyprus, Malta, and Abu Dhabi.

Wallet Irregularities

Investigators identified clusters of wallets tied to Kessler Corp that engaged in circular transfers, timed movements before regulatory deadlines, and interactions with offshore exchanges known for minimal KYC enforcement.

Corporate Complexity

Subsidiaries appeared and dissolved rapidly. Board members rotated frequently, with several having no digital footprint. Analysts noted that many entities were incorporated in jurisdictions known for flexible disclosure requirements.

The UAE Connection

Dubai served as the operational heart of Kessler’s empire. He attended private fintech gatherings, often accompanied by unidentified advisors. Witnesses described him as charismatic but increasingly guarded.

The Disappearance

In early 2026, after a closed-door meeting in Abu Dhabi, Kessler abruptly vanished. Corporate filings ceased, wallet activity stopped, and several subsidiaries dissolved quietly. His whereabouts remain unknown.
